# ☕ Coffee Shop Sales Dashboard — Power BI

An interactive **Power BI** dashboard analyzing **2,000 coffee-shop transactions** across
5 Indian cities — built to surface what sells, where, and how customers pay.

## 📊 The dashboard
> Open `Coffee_Shop_Sales.pbix` in Power BI Desktop, or view `dashboard.png`.
> Don't have the .pbix yet? Build it in ~30 minutes with **[BUILD_GUIDE.md](BUILD_GUIDE.md)** — the dataset is included.

## 🔑 Key insights (from the data)
- **₹551,130** total sales · **2,000** transactions · **₹276** average transaction value
- **Coffee drives ~50% of revenue** (₹278K) — Latte is the #1 product
- **UPI is the dominant payment method (~56%)**, ahead of Card and Cash
- Sales are spread fairly evenly across cities, led slightly by **Pune**

## 🧩 What it shows
- KPI cards: total sales, transactions, quantity, average transaction value
- Monthly sales **trend**, sales by **category**, sales by **city**
- **Payment-method** mix and **top products**
- Interactive **slicers** (city, category, date)
